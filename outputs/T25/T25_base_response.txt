### How to interpret the chart

- Positive correlation: Countries with a higher share of older people tend to have more cumulative COVID-19 deaths per million.
- Negative correlation: Countries with a higher share of older people tend to have fewer cumulative deaths per million.
- Correlation near zero: There is little evidence of a linear relationship in the data.

The red line shows the overall trend, while the correlation coefficient ranges from −1 to +1.

Important limitation: This chart can reveal an association, but it cannot establish that an older population causes higher COVID-19 death rates. Healthcare quality, vaccination, reporting practices, testing, and the timing of outbreaks can also affect the results. The cumulative death rates are taken from each country’s latest available record, so the reporting dates may differ.
