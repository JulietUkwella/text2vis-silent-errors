The code below compares the median cumulative reported COVID-19 deaths per million people in low-income versus high-income countries. It uses pandas for data processing and matplotlib for the chart.

Because your CSV does not include an income-group column, it retrieves income classifications from the World Bank API and matches countries using their ISO codes.&#x20;

## How to interpret the chart

- Lower bar for low-income countries: Their median reported deaths per million were lower.
- Higher bar for low-income countries: Their median reported deaths per million were higher.
- Numbers above the bars: The median death rate and the number of countries included in each group.

The median is used so that countries with very large populations or unusually high death rates do not dominate the comparison. The rate is based on reported deaths, not all actual deaths; differences in reporting and death registration can affect comparisons.&#x20;
