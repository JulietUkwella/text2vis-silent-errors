## How to interpret the chart

Look at how the red line (the seven-day average of new deaths) changes as the blue line (vaccination coverage) rises.

- If deaths fall as vaccination coverage increases, the chart is consistent with vaccines having contributed to reduced mortality.
- If deaths rise or show peaks despite increasing coverage, other factors, such as new variants, seasonal effects, and infection waves, may also be influencing deaths.
- Important: This chart shows a relationship over time, not proof that vaccines caused the decline. Changes in age-specific vaccination, prior immunity, public-health restrictions, and reporting practices also matter.

Conclusion: The chart lets you investigate whether reported daily deaths fell during the vaccine rollout. The actual conclusion should be based on the plotted data; the code does not assume in advance that deaths declined.
