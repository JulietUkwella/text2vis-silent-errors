## 1. Analytical choices to make before creating the chart

The question “Which countries vaccinated the most people against COVID?” has several possible interpretations. I would make the following choices.

| Analytical choice        | Decision                                                                                                                                                             | Reason                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Measure of vaccination   | Use `people_vaccinated`.                                                                                                                                             | This measures the cumulative number of people who received at least one vaccine dose, rather than the total number of doses administered.                                    |
| Absolute totals or rates | Use absolute totals.                                                                                                                                                 | The question asks which countries vaccinated the most people, so the number of people is the most direct measure.                                                            |
| Time period              | Use the latest available non-missing cumulative value for each country.                                                                                              | Vaccination totals accumulate over time, so using the latest reported value gives a useful overall comparison without adding together cumulative counts from multiple dates. |
| Which rows to include    | Include country and territory entries with three-letter ISO codes, plus Kosovo (`OWID_KOS`). Exclude aggregate entries such as World, continents, and income groups. | Aggregate rows could otherwise appear alongside individual countries and distort the ranking.                                                                                |
| Missing data             | Ignore rows with missing vaccination counts or invalid dates.                                                                                                        | Missing values cannot provide a reliable ranking.                                                                                                                            |
| Number of countries      | Display the top 10.                                                                                                                                                  | A horizontal bar chart makes the leading countries easy to compare and keeps country labels readable.                                                                        |

### What could make the chart misleading?

There are several important limitations:

- Population size matters. Countries with larger populations will generally have higher vaccination totals. This does not mean they achieved higher vaccination coverage. For a coverage comparison, use `people_vaccinated_per_hundred` instead.
- At least one dose is not the same as full vaccination. The selected measure includes people who received only one dose. To compare completed vaccination, use `people_fully_vaccinated`.
- Reporting dates can differ. One country's latest observation might be much older than another's. The chart will include each country's latest non-missing date in its label to make this easier to see.
- Reporting may be incomplete. Some countries have missing observations, delayed reporting, or revised historical figures. The latest reported value is not necessarily a perfectly comparable or fully up-to-date count.
- Countries and territories are not identical categories. The dataset includes some territories as well as countries, so the ranking should be interpreted as a comparison of country/territory entries.
- Cumulative counts must not be summed over dates. Doing so would count the same vaccinated people repeatedly and produce incorrect totals.

With these decisions, the chart will answer: Which 10 countries or territories have the highest reported cumulative number of people who received at least one COVID-19 vaccine dose?

## 3. How to interpret the result

Each bar represents a country's or territory's latest reported cumulative count of people who received at least one COVID-19 vaccine dose. The longest bar identifies the largest reported count among the included entries.

The date beside each country helps reveal whether the observations are from comparable reporting periods. The values at the ends of the bars use M for millions and B for billions.

One final consideration: if your real goal is to determine which countries performed best at getting their populations vaccinated, a second chart using `people_vaccinated_per_hundred` would be more appropriate. It would compare the share of the population vaccinated rather than rewarding countries simply for having larger populations.
