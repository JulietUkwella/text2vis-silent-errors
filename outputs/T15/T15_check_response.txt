The code filters for 2023, drops missing index values, and requires at least 30 reporting days. Consequently, it could exclude most countries or calculate averages from only a small portion of the year. Those partial-period averages would not reliably compare how strict COVID restrictions were across countries throughout 2023.

To fix it: Use a dataset with sufficiently complete policy data for 2023, or change the comparison period to a year adequately covered by the Stringency Index data.
